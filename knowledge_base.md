# University of Manitoba Knowledge Base

**Q: How do I apply to University of Manitoba?**
A: You can apply through the UofM admissions portal at umanitoba.ca/admissions. Deadlines vary by program.

**Q: What programs does UofM offer?**
A: UofM offers programs in Engineering, Science, Arts, Business, Medicine, Education, and more.

**Q: What is the tuition fee at UofM?**
A: Domestic students pay approximately $4,000–$5,500 per year. International students pay higher rates depending on the program.

**Q: Is student housing available at UofM?**
A: Yes, UofM offers on-campus residences. Apply early as spots fill quickly at umanitoba.ca/student-residences.

**Q: What support is available for international students?**
A: UofM has an International Centre offering visa help, orientation, academic support, and cultural programs.
