# University of Manitoba Knowledge Base

**Q: How do I apply to University of Manitoba?**
A: You can apply through the UofM admissions portal at umanitoba.ca/admissions. Deadlines vary by program.

**Q: What programs does UofM offer?**
A: UofM offers programs in Engineering, Science, Arts, Business, Medicine, Education, and more.

**Q: What is the tuition fee at UofM?**
A: Domestic students pay approximately $4,000–$5,500 per year. International students pay higher rates depending on the program.

**Q: Is student housing available at UofM?**
A: Yes, UofM offers on-campus residences. Apply early as spots fill quickly at umanitoba.ca/student-residences.

**Q: What support is available for international students**
A: UofM has an International Centre offering visa help, orientation, academic support, and cultural programs.

**Q: What support is available for international students?**
A: UM's International Centre offers orientation programs, immigration advising, health coverage information, a Weekly Conversation Group, a Winter Clothing Library, and intercultural experience programs. They can be reached at international@umanitoba.ca or 204-474-8501.

**Q: How do I pay my tuition?**
A: Within Canada, you can pay via mobile/online banking (set up UM as a payee using your student number), debit at the Student Accounting Office, cheque/money order, or credit card through Aurora (note: a 2.5% fee applies for credit card payments).

**Q: How do I get my student ID card?**
A: Join the queue online via Waitwell, then bring your government-issued photo ID, study permit (if applicable), and student number to your appointment. The ID Centre will take your photo, record your signature, and print your card on the spot.

**Q: What scholarships and bursaries are available at UM?**
A: UM offers a wide range of scholarships and bursaries for new, current, and graduate students, including options specific to Indigenous and International students. The entrance scholarship deadline is March 1st, and the Fall/Winter General Bursary application deadline is October 1st. Contact Financial Aid and Awards at awards@umanitoba.ca for more information.

**Q: How do I order a transcript?**
A: Log into Aurora, search "Order a Document" in the Find Cards bar, then choose between an unofficial or official transcript and select your delivery option. Alumni without Aurora access can email transcripts@umanitoba.ca.

**Q: What science student groups can I join at UM?**
A: The Faculty of Science has groups across many disciplines, including the Science Students' Association (SSA), Biology Undergraduate Students' Association (BUGS), Computer Science Students' Association (CSSA), EigenClub (math), OPUS (physics), Undergraduate Psychology Student Association (UPSA), and many more.

**Q: What programs does the Faculty of Science offer?**
A: The Faculty of Science offers undergraduate BSc programs (including Actuarial Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Biochemistry, Biological Sciences, and more), as well as Master's and PhD programs in all seven departments: Biological Sciences, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics, Microbiology, Physics and Astronomy, and Statistics.

**Q: How can I appeal or adjust my tuition fees?**
A: Fees can be adjusted by withdrawing before the revision period deadline, through Authorized Withdrawals, or via a successful appeal. Fee appeals are submitted through Aurora. If you no longer have Aurora access, contact Fee.Appeals@umanitoba.ca.

**Q: Where is the Financial Aid and Awards office located?**
A: Room 422 University Centre. You can also reach them at 204-474-9531 or awards@umanitoba.ca. Office hours are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and Wednesday 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

**Q: Can I get a student loan as an international student?**
A: Government student loans are available to full-time Canadian citizens and permanent residents. Students from the US may apply for US government loan programs or alternative loan sources.

**Q: What is the Work-Study Program?**
A: It's a part-time, on-campus employment program for students with financial need. To be eligible, you must have proof of a 2025-2026 government student aid package of at least $2,000 (or equivalent Indigenous funding), be a full-time undergraduate or graduate student, have a minimum 2.0 GPA, and be residing in Manitoba during the employment term.

**Q: How do I request a refund?**
A: Approved refunds can be paid by e-transfer, cheque, or credit card (note: the convenience fee is non-refundable). For payments made from outside Canada, refunds are issued through the original payment method. Contact refund_request@umanitoba.ca for questions.

**Q: How do I view my tuition fees?**
A: Log into Aurora and use the "View Account Summary by Term" card. Tuition amounts are available after course registration is complete.

**Q: Can I opt out of the student health and dental insurance plan?**
A: Yes, during the Change-of-Coverage period for each term. You must provide proof of equivalent existing coverage (e.g., through a parent, spouse, or band council). Manitoba Health coverage alone is not considered equivalent. Approved opt-outs remain valid for the full policy year.

**Q: How do I request a payment extension for tuition?**
A: Send a request to StudentPayment@umanitoba.ca before the payment deadline.

**Q: Can I pay tuition by credit card?**
A: Yes, through Aurora, but a 2.5% convenience fee applies. The Cashier's Office does not accept credit card payments directly.

**Q: What is Aurora and what can I use it for?**
A: Aurora is UM's online student portal. You can use it to register for classes, view and purchase course materials, view your tuition fees, view your final grades, order official documents, and appeal fees.

**Q: How do I get my diploma if I missed convocation?**
A: Fill out the Parchment Delivery Request Form and email it to Registrar.Office@umanitoba.ca using your student email. Your diploma will be mailed to your provided address approximately 2 weeks after the last ceremony, or you can request a PDF copy.

**Q: What if I need a replacement diploma?**
A: Email Graduation.Convocation@umanitoba.ca using your student email. Your replacement will be mailed within 10 business days, or a PDF copy can be requested.

**Q: How do I order an enrolment verification?**
A: Log into Aurora, search "Order a Document," and select "Enrolment Verification Request." Choose your term(s), type of verification, and number of copies. Standard mail costs $13.50 per term per copy, and Email Next Day Service costs $34.00 per term per copy.

**Q: Where is the Registrar's Office?**
A: 400 UMSU University Centre, Fort Garry campus. Phone: 204-474-9420 (or toll-free 1-877-474-9420). Hours are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and Wednesday 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

**Q: What does the International Centre's McCall MacBain Fellowship offer?**
A: It provides up to $30,000 per fellowship for globally minded UM students to live, work, and study abroad for a full year. It recognizes students who show leadership potential, community engagement, and strong character.

**Q: Is there a space for international students to hang out on campus?**
A: Yes, the Tony T.K. Lau Global Lounge is a relaxing space for students to study, connect, and learn about opportunities through the International Centre. It also has rooftop patio access and can be booked for student and staff events.

**Q: What undergraduate science programs are available?**
A: Programs include Actuarial Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Biochemistry, and Biological Sciences, among others. The Faculty of Science offers a wide range of BSc degrees across its seven departments.

**Q: Does UM offer co-op programs for science students?**
A: Yes, the Science Co-op program lets students earn paid work experience while completing their undergraduate degree, helping build their resume and professional network.

**Q: What is the General Science Internship Program (GSIP)?**
A: GSIP offers students paid work experience through an internship, helping them develop professional skills, expand their network, and explore career options. It is open to international students as well.

**Q: What is the Wawatay Project?**
A: Wawatay (Anishinaabe for Northern Lights) is a zero-cost initiative supporting Indigenous students in the Faculty of Science. It provides a full range of support services, cultural connection, and research engagement opportunities.

**Q: Where can a computer science student get involved on campus?**
A: CS students can join the Computer Science Students' Association (CSSA) at E1-586 EITC (cssa@umanitoba.ca), Women in Computer Science Council (UMWICS), or devClub, which focuses on hands-on coding experience with various engines and software.
